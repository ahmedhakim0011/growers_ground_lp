Why Growers Ground

Three Core Capabilities

One platform. Two audiences. Community built in — not bolted on.

Map-First Discovery

AI-ranked pins surface the most relevant hosts or gardeners near you — with transparent "why this match" chips. Tap a pin, view a profile, connect.

Community Garden Spots

Pin seed swaps, compost sites, water access, and community plots — confirmed by local growers. Public-good content, separate from private host listings.

Connect & Grow Together

Real-time messaging, growing journals, neighborhood groups, seasonal events, and local Q&A — everything tied to where you actually garden.

Simple by Design

How Growers Ground Works

Three steps to ground. No training required.

  1. 1

    Download & Create Your Profile

    Sign up as a gardener or host. Add photos, your growing experience, and what you're looking for — or what ground you have to offer.

  2. 2

    Discover on the Map

    Browse AI-ranked matches in your area. Filter by distance, save favourites, and explore community garden spots on a separate map layer.

  3. 3

    Connect & Start Growing

    Message, agree on rent or barter terms, and join the community — journals, groups, seed swaps, and local events.
