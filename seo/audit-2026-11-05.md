# SEO audit and traffic plan (2026-11-05)

Method: instructions from [claude-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo) (audit, programmatic and quality-gate rules), applied by hand. Its installer and scripts were not run. Evidence: Search Console screenshots from 2026-11-05, a crawl of the live site, and the code in this repo.

**Not measured:** Core Web Vitals (PageSpeed API quota exceeded), backlinks (no data source), current indexed-page count (last known: 17 of 69 on 2026-10-24). No 0-100 health score is given because those inputs are missing.

## Where the site is

| Metric | Last 7 days | Per day | Goal per day |

| Impressions | 133 | 19 | 1,000 |
| Clicks | 3 | 0.4 | 200 |

**The goal's ratio needs changing.** 200 clicks from 1,000 impressions is a 20% CTR. That normally takes mostly #1-#2 rankings or brand searches. Today's CTR is 2.3%. At a 3-4% CTR, 200 clicks a day needs about 5,000-7,000 impressions a day, roughly 300 times today's volume. Recommended goal: **200 clicks a day, tracked through impressions as the leading number.**

**Keyword demand may not support it yet.** In `keywords.csv`, the only term above 1K monthly US searches is "community gardens near me" (10K-100K), and maps and local results are likely to take much of that traffic. Measured city terms are 100-1K a month at best. 200 clicks a day is about 6,000 a month, so the plan has to widen what the site can rank for, not just polish these 49 URLs.

## Findings, most important first

1. **High: garden names were not in the visible page.** City pages showed "Loading map…", with names only inside a script payload. 642 real garden names were effectively not page content. Search Console already shows a garden-name query ("four winds allotment gardens"). **Fixed on this branch.**
2. **High: coverage is weakest in the biggest markets.** New York 18 mapped gardens, Los Angeles 20, Houston 3, Dallas-Fort Worth 1, Atlanta 1, Miami 0. Detroit has 150. Only 642 of the 1,329 pins on indexable pages have a real name. This is the largest growth lever. **Open.**
3. **High: authority is probably low.** The domain has data only from September 2026, and no link building is recorded. Backlinks are unmeasured. **Open.**
4. **High: possible trust problem on the homepage.** "Thousands of growers are planning ahead", with quotes from "early members", appears on a site whose store badges lead to a waitlist. If those numbers and quotes are not real, they should come down. **Needs owner confirmation.** "Download on the App Store" badges that open a waitlist have the same issue.
5. **Medium: city pages were close to the skill's thin-page limit.** Live pages run about 380 words against a 500-600 target for location pages. The garden lists and nearby links help; cities with few named gardens still fall short: Los Angeles, Hamilton and Cincinnati have 2 each, Charlotte and Cleveland 4.
6. **Medium: no links between city pages.** Each city linked only to the directory. **Fixed: five nearest cities.**
7. **Medium: render-blocking Trustpilot script on every page**, for a footer widget. **Fixed: loads after the page; widget confirmed rendering.**
8. **Medium: Nashville has no local section.** One search found no official source for how plots work, so nothing was written. San José was added from the city's own page. **Nashville is open.**
9. **Low: sitemap `lastmod` is not page-specific.** Static and city pages share the data-sync time, which Google tends to ignore. **Open.**

Working well: crawl access, canonical www redirect, static caching, sitemap, breadcrumb and article schema, noindex on thin cities, and specific local sections on 32 of 33 cities after this branch.

## Changed on `seo/city-page-garden-lists`

## Roadmap

The targets below are planning targets, not forecasts. Gardening search is also seasonal, so a slow winter is expected and spring is when this should show most.

| By | Work | Target per day |
|---|---|---|
| Now - Dec 2026 | Deploy this branch; request indexing for the top 10 cities; resolve finding 4; Nashville section; check the Pages report | 50 impressions, 1-2 clicks |
| Jan - Feb 2027 | Add open-data gardens for New York, Los Angeles, Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Atlanta, Miami; publish remaining guides before the season; start outreach to garden organizations and local press | 200 impressions, 5-8 clicks |
| Mar - May 2027 | First 50 individual garden pages, only where an official source gives address and contact details (San José's 21 are a good start); review results for 2-4 weeks before adding more | 1,000 impressions, 30-40 clicks |
| Jun - Nov 2027 | Expand what worked, in batches of 50-100; refresh weak pages from Search Console data; keep earning links | 5,000+ impressions, 150-200 clicks |

Rule from the skill worth keeping: never generate pages that only swap a city or garden name. With 60 metros the site is already past its location-page warning level, so each new page needs real local facts.

## Review checkpoints

Check Search Console every two weeks: impressions, indexed pages, queries that contain garden names, and city-page position. If impressions are below 50 a day by mid-January after indexing is healthy, look at authority first.
