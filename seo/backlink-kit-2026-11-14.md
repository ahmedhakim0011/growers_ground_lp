## Ready-to-paste copy

**Name:** Growers Ground
**URL:** https://www.growersground.com/
**Tagline (60):** Garden sharing app: find garden space or share your yard
**Short description (160):** Growers Ground maps people with unused yards and gardeners who need space to grow, plus community gardens and seed swaps, across the US and Canada.
**Long description:**
> Lots of people want to grow vegetables but have no yard. Lots of homeowners have yards they never use. Growers Ground is a map-first garden sharing (yard sharing) app that connects the two. Hosts list unused backyard space with an approximate location; gardeners browse the map, read profiles and message before anyone commits. A second map layer shows community spots: seed swaps, tool libraries, compost drops and garden events. Hosts and gardeners agree their own terms (harvest share, help or rent); Growers Ground does not handle payments. The app is launching city by city with a waitlist; the website already has free community garden maps for 35 metros and guides on finding and sharing garden space.
**Categories / tags:** gardening, community, sharing economy, maps, sustainability, local, food
**Alternatives to list against:** YardSharing, Shared Earth, Sowmate, Garden Neighbors
